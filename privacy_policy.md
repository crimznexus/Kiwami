# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Kiwami is a free, open-source app (GPL-3.0) published at https://github.com/crimznexus/Kiwami. It has no accounts of its own, no analytics, and no advertising. This page explains what data stays on your device and what leaves it.

## What stays on your device

- Your AniList and MyAnimeList sign-in tokens, your settings, your reading and watching progress, and your downloads are stored only on your phone. Kiwami does not send them to the developer or to any server run by the developer.
- The AI page enhancer runs entirely on your phone. Pages are never uploaded.

## What the app contacts

- **AniList and MyAnimeList**, when you sign in, to load and update your lists. Their own privacy policies apply: https://anilist.co/terms and https://myanimelist.net/about/privacy_policy.
- **GitHub**, to check for a newer Kiwami release and to download it. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request.
- **The extension sources you install.** Extensions are made by third parties and contact their own websites to load manga and anime. Those sites can see your IP address and what you open. Kiwami does not control them; install only extensions you trust.
- **Discord**, only if you turn on Discord Rich Presence, to show what you are watching.

## Crash reports

This build does not send crash reports. If the app crashes it can save a log file on your device; it is shared only if you choose to send it.

## Children

Kiwami is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published in this file with a new date.
